## Fades, tapers, and everything between

"Fade" isn't one haircut — it's a family of them. A skin fade, a low fade, a taper, a scissor cut with a tapered neckline: these are different techniques for different hair types and different amounts of maintenance you want to sign up for. Tell your barber what you're picturing (a photo helps more than the word "fade" alone) and they'll tell you honestly whether that works with your hair.
